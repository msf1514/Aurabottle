Support FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the self-cleaning technology work?

The Aura Bottle uses built-in UV-C LED technology (280nm wavelength) inside the cap to destroy the DNA of odor-causing bacteria and bio-contaminants, purifying both the water and the inner bottle surfaces.

Does the bottle require filters?

No filters are required! The UV-C purification cycle kills germs without physically filtering particulate matter, so it never needs expensive filter replacements.

How long does the battery last?

A key benefit of Aura Bottle is its extreme efficiency. A single charge via the included magnetic USB-C cable will power the bottle for up to 30 days of normal self-cleaning cycles.

Is the bottle dishwasher safe?

The bottle chamber itself is top-rack dishwasher safe. However, because the cap contains the UV-C LED battery and electronics, it should only be hand-washed under warm running water.
